# Haven ☁️

**A little space for you to feel it, name it, and let it out.**

🔗 **Live app:** https://ruquiyaarsheen26-rgb.github.io/haven/

---

## About
Haven is a calm, privacy-first web app for people who want somewhere safe to untangle their feelings. There are no accounts, no servers and no scores. Everything is saved only in your own browser.

Haven isn't a therapist or an emergency service. It's a soft place to pause, notice and breathe.

## Features
- 😊 **Mood check-ins:** pick how you feel and save it
- 📓 **Journal:** write freely, with gentle prompts to get you started
- ✦ **Mood board:** collect words, wishes and tiny goals
- 🌷 **Weekly check-in:** a no-pressure look back at your week
- 💛 **Support page:** kind prompts to help you pause and think
- 🔒 **Private:** your entries never leave your device
- 📱 **Responsive:** works on phones, tablets and laptops

## Built with
HTML5 · CSS3 · JavaScript · localStorage

## How to use
1. Open the live link above.
2. Check in with your mood on the Home page.
3. Write in your journal or add something to your mood board.
4. Visit the Weekly check-in to look back on your week.

## Note
Entries are saved in the browser on the device you use, so they don't sync between devices. Clearing your browser data will delete them.
